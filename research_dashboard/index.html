WHAT EACH AGENT DOES

Controller — coordinates the research pipeline and checkpoints. It does not approve live trading.

FETCH — collects historical market data needed for tests.

ARCHIVIST — investigates missing histories for companies that left the index.

ORACLE — investigates ticker changes, mergers, acquisitions, spinoffs, and related identity changes.

CIPHER — diagnoses provider failures and possible approved recovery sources.

PULSE — looks for duplicated work, stalls, and inefficient research steps.

JARVIS — performs an independent research/audit pass under its own safety policy.

WATCHDOG — safety stop/check before experimental strategy work proceeds.

VISION — runs strategy experiments and cross-sectional research on validated data.

ULTRON — adversarially stress-tests results, looking for weaknesses or failure cases rather than trying to make them look good.

EDITH — records completion/evidence from the research pipeline.

GATEKEEPER — evaluates the final evidence against the fixed acceptance rules.

WHAT THE PIPELINE IS DOING

The pipeline is the ordered checklist B27B follows: security checks → membership/data checks → historical-data collection and repair → ticker/corporate-action investigation → universe audit → independent JARVIS audit → WATCHDOG safety check → VISION experiments → ULTRON stress testing → EDITH evidence record → GATEKEEPER review → optimizer summary. A later stage does not get credit until it is actually reached and completed.

PROJECT B27B — OVERALL GOAL

WHY THIS SYSTEM EXISTS

Build a research engine that can determine whether a trading strategy has earned the right to move forward.

Project B27B is a research-first trading-system project. Its job is to collect and validate historical market data, reconstruct the stocks that actually existed in the historical universe, test strategies without changing the rules to manufacture a good result, challenge those results with independent audits and adversarial stress tests, and preserve evidence of what passed and what failed.

The goal is not to force B27B to become profitable or to guarantee returns. The goal is to find out, as honestly as possible, whether any strategy demonstrates repeatable performance after realistic costs and survives the project's fixed data-quality, validation, safety, and Gatekeeper requirements.

GOOD DATA→FAIR TESTS→STRESS TESTS→INDEPENDENT REVIEW→EVIDENCE-BASED DECISION

Current operating mode: RESEARCH / PAPER ONLY. The dashboard reports evidence and progress; it does not authorize or place real-money trades.
