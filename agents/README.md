# B27B autonomous research layer

This branch adds the persistent research control plane. It is intentionally paper-only.

Roles:
- JARVIS: schedules bounded research work.
- FETCH: validates data inputs.
- VISION: proposes paper experiments.
- FRIDAY: reviews completed results.
- ULTRON: adversarial/risk review.
- EDITH: audit/reporting.
- WATCHDOG: health/recovery.
- GATEKEEPER: enforces agent_policy.json.

No role may enable live orders, sign transactions, withdraw funds, or modify the governing policy.
